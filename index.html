At this pace my estimate for Jan 2 is roughly 605 to 645. The Jan 23 retake, plus the Superscore, is your realistic shot at 675. Your 515 came from a cold first attempt, so your real starting point is probably somewhat higher.

Two ways to close the gap: study 3 hours a day over winter break (Dec 19 to Jan 1 adds about 21 hours), and treat the practice exams as gates. If Exams 5 and 6 both come in under 635, move Test 1 back.

The weekly shape

Half your time goes to Quant

Quant is your weakest section by far, and Data Insights uses the same math, so drilling Quant lifts both. From Phase 3 on, the learning days become full timed sections, but the weekly shape stays the same.

What to use

Target Test Prep (Quant and Data Insights)

Your main course. The Quant topics in this plan follow its chapters. It bills monthly, so keep the pace up.

GMAT Official Guide 2025–2026

Real retired questions for timed sets and all Verbal work. Use the online question bank to build custom timed sets.

Official Practice Exams (mba.com)

Exams 1 and 2 are free; 3 to 6 are sold together. This plan assumes your diagnostic was Exam 1. If it wasn't, take Exam 1 on Oct 24 and shift the rest.

GMAT Club forum

Free expert explanations for nearly every OG question. Search the first line of a question to find its thread.

Your error log

A spreadsheet with date, source, section, topic, why you missed it (concept, careless, timing), and the fix. Every Sunday runs on it.
